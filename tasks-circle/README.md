# Loose Tasks

One floating circle for your to-do list and your ideas. Tap the circle from any app: the task list opens
(minus removes, plus adds), with a second box below to save an idea. Tap outside to close. Ideas are listed
in the app. Everything is saved on the phone, and the circle starts again by itself after a restart.

The small text strip at the top of the popup has a pencil icon: tap it to change the text (saved on the phone).
